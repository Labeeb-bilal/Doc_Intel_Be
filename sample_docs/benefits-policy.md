# Benefits Policy

## Vacation Accrual

Full-time employees accrue 15 vacation days annually, credited at the start of each calendar year. Unused days do not carry over and are forfeited at year end unless local law requires otherwise.

## Health Insurance

Full-time employees become eligible for company health insurance after 90 days of employment. Coverage begins on the first day of the month following the eligibility date.

## Confidentiality

Employees must not disclose confidential company information to third parties without written consent. This obligation continues for a period of two years after termination of employment.
